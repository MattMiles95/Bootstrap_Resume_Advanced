About

How I work

I offer a warm, collaborative space to explore what’s troubling you at your own pace. Sessions are confidential and tailored to your needs, drawing on contemporary, evidence‑informed approaches.

Who I help

Adults seeking support with anxiety, low mood, stress and burnout, life changes, self‑esteem, relationships, and feeling stuck. Short‑term or open‑ended therapy available.

What to expect

An initial consultation to understand your goals, followed by weekly 50‑minute sessions. We’ll regularly review progress together.

Services

Individual Therapy

Weekly, 50‑minute sessions. In‑person (London) or secure video. Short‑term (6–12 sessions) or open‑ended.

Online Therapy

Therapy from the comfort of your home via encrypted video platform. UK residents only.
